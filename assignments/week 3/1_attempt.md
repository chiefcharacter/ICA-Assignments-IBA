E) As it can be seen, the mean with the flagged value is very high, and the mean without the flagged value is much lower, however the median for botht the flagged value and the unflagged value are the exact same.

This difference is caused because mean is influenced by outliers or extreme values, however median is never influenced by extreme outliers.

This is because median is the exact middle value, but mean is the average of all the values. This is why is theres an extreme outlier, a very big gap between the 2nd last and last values, the total average gets disturbed but the middle value stays the same.

Hence, if there are extreme outliers, median is the better approach to find the right price, but if there aren't any outliers and we need accurate representative value for our data, than mean is the better approach.
